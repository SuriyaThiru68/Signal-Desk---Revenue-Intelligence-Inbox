## Analysis

### Migration blocked by credit expectation and unconfirmed bank-card integration

**Confidence:** high · **Analysis version:** analysis-2026-08-22.1 · **Analysis source revision:** 1

#### What happened

Cedarwave Distribution discussed migrating its existing card programme. The replacement remains prepaid, while the customer's main issue is moving to credit. SpendNest described a possible bank-issued corporate credit-card feed, but existing-card eligibility, available-limit visibility and accounting reconciliation were not confirmed. A separate department-user cleanup was also requested.

#### Why it matters

This is an existing-customer retention and support risk, not a clean expansion. The customer may keep credit cards at its bank and use SpendNest only for receipts and review. Promising credit, limit visibility or accounting sync before the programme team confirms would overstate the product.

#### Evidence

- **Turn 10** — “The replacement I described is still prepaid. I do not want to promise that this becomes a credit card in the near term.”
  The rep avoided overpromising credit.
- **Turn 11** — “moving from prepaid to credit is the main issue for us.”
  The customer's primary decision criterion.
- **Turn 19** — “It is not the same as our real-time prepaid feed.”
  The bank-card feed would behave differently from prepaid.
- **Turn 21** — “Many transaction fields can sync, but source-balance reconciliation is a question in this setup.”
  Accounting reconciliation is unresolved.
- **Turn 23** — “I will check whether your already-issued cards can join the transaction-feed programme.”
  Existing-card eligibility is not confirmed.
- **Turn 25** — “No, the proposed feed shows transaction details after settlement.”
  The feed would not show available credit limit.
- **Turn 27** — “The additional layer is collecting receipts and business reasons, reviewing exceptions, attaching project information and reconciling alongside prepaid spending.”
  The remaining value proposition if the bank keeps credit control.
- **Turn 30** — “There is also the earlier issue with moving users between departments.”
  A separate operational cleanup request.

#### What the evidence does not establish

- Bank approval, existing-card eligibility and network consent are not confirmed.
- Accounting reconciliation for bank-card transactions is not confirmed.
- The customer has not decided on a mixed credit/prepaid model or completed migration.

#### Coaching notes

- When a migration involves a product limitation, lead with the limitation and the customer's decision criteria.
- Do not treat an existing customer's migration discussion as expansion until the replacement product actually meets the stated need.
- Keep bank-card eligibility, limit visibility and accounting sync as separate confirmations; one does not imply the others.
